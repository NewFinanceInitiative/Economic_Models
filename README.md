# Economic Models

The Bitcoin standard & economic models for the Ethereum blockchain.

## Bitcoin Economic Model

The Bitcoin economic model is developed to be a simulated economic activity processor. The idea is based with the notion of understanding the Bitcoin Standard and how Bitcoin natively operates under real world and simulated stress tested scenarios. The point is to give consumers a fundamental blockchain economic model that shows how applied transactions with real financial value determine the health and state of the Bitcoin network and natively $BTC as an underlying currency that is consumed for the purchase of real world goods and services and how $BTC's relative price can be defined with a uint account measurement on 1 BTC.

The opportunity to develop refined and authentic models for the Bitcoin network is possible. This means that developing a Bitcoin economic model for the simulation of treasury management can be achieved. The instance of a national treasury Bitcoin economic model, can take into account economics zones like the USA, Canada, UK, UAE and other trading partners, to create an understanding of applied economics to show how affective using Bitcoin is for managing treasuries and determining the health of economic zones like the USA and other trading partners. 

There is possibilities that such Bitcoin economic models can be developed to what ever kind of economic landscape or activity is used for the exchange of value with goods and services. Pokèmon is a great example of how creative development and a Bitcoin economic model can be used to stress and test the proprietary economics that are native to Bitcoin and $BTC - it means that using code and the Bitcoin standard to create an economy that enables the sale and purchase of Pokèmon cards in Bitcoin specifically can be modelled to show how just the economics of Bitcoin function in real world scenarios that transact and send financial value. 

## Ethereum Economic Models

The based point of programmability in Ethereum is a massive advancement in technology capabilities for economists and financial technology. 

The New Finance Initiative economic models warehoused in "Escentra" are based with understanding different types of economic activity that happen on the Ethereum blockchain. As a native economy that operates with consumers and producers, RebusMatic is developed to operate as a neo-classical austrian consumer & producer theory economic model that provides an insight into how the behaviour of consumers and producers on the Ethereum network operate. RebusMatic is aimed mostly at understanding the differences between consumers and producers and how behaviour on the Ethereum blockchain is processed.

The other economic models warehoused in Escentra are developed with an incentive to understand behaviours such as influence, consumerism, objectives of activity, and scarcity on the Ethereum blockchain.
